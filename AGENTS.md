# Project Working Instructions

## Mandatory Full Devices Preview Before Push

This is a critical approval gate for the Francesco Cappuccio website.

- Before every Git push or deployment, stop and ask Francesco: **"Do you want the full devices preview before I push?"**
- "Full devices preview" means three separate, live, scrollable previews of every affected page:
  - Desktop: 1440 × 900 px viewport.
  - Tablet: 768 × 1024 px viewport.
  - Mobile: 390 × 844 px viewport.
- Open the three versions as separate tabs in the right-side preview panel whenever the available tooling permits it.
- Do not substitute static screenshots for the three live previews unless Francesco explicitly requests screenshots.
- Review each version for composition, typography, spacing, image and video proportions, controls, horizontal overflow, and content order.
- Do not push or deploy until Francesco has reviewed the previews and explicitly approved the push.
- Device-preview helper pages are local development tools. Do not include them in a push or deployment unless Francesco explicitly asks to publish them.
